Deck Tutor

Card suggestions from the live Scryfall database

Got a pile of cards and no list? Scan them with the camera scanner, then copy what it gives you and paste it below. It reads the card names, so cards stacked with only their titles showing are fine.

You don't have to. If you already have a list, or only want to add a few cards, skip straight to the box below.

One card per line, like 1 Sol Ring or 4x Lightning Bolt. Exports from Moxfield, Archidekt, MTG Arena, ManaBox or SortSwift paste straight in, as plain text or as the comma-separated file — set codes, collector numbers and foil marks are ignored.

Or add a few cards from a photo. Best for 1 to 15 cards you'd rather not type — it finds roughly half the names in a photograph, so it is not the way to get a whole deck in.

Use the original photo from your camera roll, not a screenshot and not one that has been through a chat app. Reading happens inside your phone and is slow, a minute or two a picture. You tick off what it got right before anything joins your list.

Saved on this device only, in this browser. Nothing leaves your phone, and clearing your browsing data clears these too.

Card text, prices and popularity come from Scryfall, which gathers them from Gatherer, TCGplayer, Cardmarket and EDHREC. Commander suggestions also read EDHREC directly where it allows it. Every card links out to those sites so you can read the wider discussion yourself. Unofficial fan tool; not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.
